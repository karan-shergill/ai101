# AI Terms

| S.No | Term | Definition | Read More |
|------|------|------------|-----------|
| 1 | LLM — Large Language Model | An LLM is a very well-read AI that learned how to talk by reading most of the internet — and now predicts the best words to respond to you. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/llm.md) |
| 2 | Tokenisation | Tokenisation is the process of chopping your text into small bite-sized pieces (tokens) that an LLM can actually understand and process. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/tokenisation.md) |
| 3 | Vectorisation | Vectorisation turns words into lists of numbers so that the computer can understand meaning mathematically — and words with similar meanings end up with similar numbers. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/vectorisation.md) |
| 4 | Attention | Attention is how the LLM figures out which words are connected to which — so it understands that "it" means "the animal", not "the street". | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/attention.md) |
| 5 | Self-Supervised Learning | Self-supervised learning is how an LLM teaches itself by playing a fill-in-the-blank game on billions of internet sentences — no human labelling needed. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/self_supervised_learning.md) |
| 6 | Transformer | A Transformer is the architectural blueprint of modern AI — it stacks multiple layers of attention and reasoning on top of each other to understand and generate language with remarkable depth. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/transformer.md) |
| 7 | Fine-tuning | Fine-tuning takes a general-purpose LLM and gives it specialised training on a focused dataset — turning a generalist into an expert for a specific task or behaviour. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/fine_tuning.md) |
| 8 | Few-shot Prompting | Few-shot prompting means giving the AI 2–5 examples of what you want inside your prompt, so it learns your exact pattern and follows it — no training required. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/few_shot_prompting.md) |
| 9 | Retrieval Augmented Generation | RAG lets an LLM look up relevant information from external documents before answering — making it more accurate, up-to-date, and trustworthy than relying on memory alone. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/retrieval_augmented_generation.md) |
| 10 | Vector Database | A Vector Database stores text as vectors (numbers) and retrieves the most meaningfully similar ones to a query — making it the backbone of semantic search and RAG systems. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/vector_database.md) |
| 11 | MCP — Model Context Protocol | MCP is a universal standard protocol that lets AI models connect to any external tool or service — giving AI the ability to take real actions in the world, not just generate text. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/mcp.md) |
| 12 | Context Engineering | Context Engineering is the discipline of carefully designing everything that goes into an AI's context window — system prompts, retrieved docs, memory, tools, and examples — so the AI has exactly what it needs to perform brilliantly. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/context_engineering.md) |
| 13 | Agents | An AI Agent is an LLM that can plan, use tools, take actions, observe results, and loop — completing complex multi-step goals autonomously rather than just answering a single question. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/agents.md) |
| 14 | Reinforcement Learning | Reinforcement Learning is a training method where AI learns by trying actions, receiving rewards or penalties, and gradually discovering the best strategy — like training a dog, but with numbers instead of treats. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/reinforcement_learning.md) |
| 15 | Chain of Thought | Chain of Thought is a technique where you ask the AI to reason through a problem step by step before answering — dramatically improving accuracy on complex tasks by making the AI think out loud. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/chain_of_thought.md) |
| 16 | Reasoning Models | Reasoning models are AI models specifically trained — using Reinforcement Learning — to think through problems deeply before answering, exploring multiple paths, catching their own mistakes, and backtracking when needed. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/reasoning_models.md) |
| 17 | Multi-modal Models | Multi-modal models are AI systems that can understand and reason across multiple types of input — text, images, audio, video, and more — all within a single unified model, just like a human brain. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/multi_modal_models.md) |
| 18 | Small Language Models | Small Language Models are intentionally compact AI models — built to run on everyday devices like phones and laptops — trading broad knowledge for speed, privacy, low cost, and the ability to work completely offline. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/small_language_models.md) |
| 19 | Distillation | Distillation is a training technique where a large powerful model (teacher) transfers its knowledge to a small efficient model (student) — not just through correct answers, but through rich probability distributions that encode how the world is structured. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/distillation.md) |
| 20 | Quantisation | Quantisation reduces the precision of a model's numbers from 32-bit decimals down to 4 or 8-bit integers — shrinking model size by up to 8x and enabling powerful AI to run on everyday devices like laptops and phones. | [Read more](https://github.com/karan-shergill/ai101/blob/main/ai_terms/quantisation.md) |
